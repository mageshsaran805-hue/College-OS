NOTIFICATION: END-SEMESTER EXAMINATIONS (FALL 2024)

Notice is hereby formally given to all matriculated scholars enrolled across B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, and Ph.D. programs that the End-Semester Theoretical and Practical Examinations for the Academic Year 2024–25 (Fall Semester) shall commence from Monday, December 02, 2024.

1. Hall Tickets & Biometric Clearance: Electronic hall tickets will be generated only for candidates possessing an active bursar financial clearance and minimum statutory biometric attendance of 75.0% as of November 25, 2024.

2. Practical Viva Schedule: Laboratory examinations and capstone viva sessions will take place prior to theoretical sittings between November 26 and November 30.
